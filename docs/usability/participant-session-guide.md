# Learner or teacher usability session guide

## Purpose

Observe whether a first-time user understands the two-step fraction task, notices what to do after feedback, and can recover without coaching. One upper-primary learner is preferred; one teacher familiar with that age group is a useful alternative.

## Time and privacy

- Allow 8–10 minutes.
- Do not collect a name, account, contact details, or identifying information.
- Do not record audio or video unless the participant or guardian has separately agreed.
- Record actions and short anonymous notes only.
- Tell the participant that the app is being tested, not them, and they may stop at any time.

## Opening script

> We are testing this fraction activity, not testing you. Please use it as you normally would. You can say what you are thinking, but I will not teach you how to complete it. You may stop whenever you want.

## Tasks

1. Hand over the app on a phone-width screen at the untouched `1/2` item.
2. Say: “Place the fraction on the number line.” Do not explain Step 1 or Step 2.
3. Let the participant continue naturally through at least three items.
4. If no error occurs, ask them on the next item to show what they think a different answer would do; do not specify which wrong answer to choose.
5. After feedback, wait to see whether they can retry without help.
6. At the end, ask: “How did the bottom and top numbers help you?”
7. Optional transfer check away from the app: ask where `2/5` would go on a 0–1 line and why. Record this separately from app completion.

## Observer rules

- Do not point at controls, define vocabulary, or tell the participant to scroll.
- If they ask for help, first ask: “What would you try next?”
- Stop after two minutes of visible frustration or whenever the participant asks.
- Note exact behaviour before interpreting its cause.

## Finding review

For each proxy finding, mark one outcome:

- **Confirmed:** directly observed behaviour supports the concern.
- **Contradicted:** observed behaviour clearly opposes the concern.
- **Inconclusive:** the session did not exercise the condition or evidence was ambiguous.

Do not infer learning effectiveness from one session. Use the result to prioritize interface changes and to describe evidence limits honestly.
